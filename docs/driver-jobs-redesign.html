Notes for the build. The route panel is vector, drawn from the pickup, drop and rider coordinates the board already has. When a stop has no pin it shows only the address and the Navigate button, as today. Every rule behind the screens is unchanged: both codes, the cash amount, held-for-you offers, location sharing, and the Navigate and Call switches in Feature access. The riders are salaried and the app has no per-job earnings, so the big number on the offer is the cash to collect, or “Prepaid” when nothing is due. There is no Decline button because the system has no decline action. While a job is active the offers list is hidden, as it is today.
