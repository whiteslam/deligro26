Every phone screen except the admin console, driven with Playwright at three phone sizes: 360×800 (budget Android, the main target for Bemetara), 375×667 (iPhone SE, short screen) and 412×915 (large Android), plus the customer app in dark mode. 92 page checks and 38 tap-through steps. The good news: the foundations are solid. Nothing overflows sideways at 360 px. Pinch-zoom is allowed. Every tab bar, sheet, filter, search box, stepper and tab works. Navigating between tabs takes 0.3–0.7 s. The rider app is the best of the four: no undersized buttons, 11 px minimum text and partly bilingual. What's left is polish that matters for this audience: bars covering content on short screens, iPhone zoom on inputs, small buttons in lists, and Hindi.

4 · Suggested fixes, in order

  1. Install card: make room for it (add its height to the page's bottom padding while shown), or show it once as a top banner. Never let it cover content.
  2. Home header: show "Home · Smriti Nagar" (label plus the locality before the PIN code), the same parsing checkout now uses.
  3. 16 px on every input (text-base) on phone screens. That alone stops iPhone zoom on all 33.
  4. Hindi, next wave: home, search, restaurant, profile, vendor menu and earnings, manager forms. Put Hindi on its own line under the English where space is tight.
  5. 44 px tap targets for dish "Add", the stepper, chips, filter pills and list actions. Keep the look by padding the tap area, not the drawing.
  6. Contrast: darken the orange behind white text (or use dark text on it), and put the storefront chips on a solid dark pill.
  7. Sheets: add role="dialog", aria-modal, a label and Escape-to-close to the item and address sheets.
  8. Raise the remaining 9–10 px chart and console text to 11 px. Drop the vendor Menu floating "+". Add <main> to the vendor phone shell. Allow two lines for names.

#Fix
1Install card and basket bar make room. The card measures how far it reaches above the bottom and publishes --install-inset; .app-scroll::after adds that much scroll room, or 76 px when the basket bar shows. When both show, the card sits above the basket bar instead of on it.
2Home header shows the label plus the locality before the PIN code (new addressLocality() helper, also used by checkout). The label no longer truncates. Bell and profile buttons are 44 px. "Deliver to · पहुंचाएं".
3No iPhone zoom: one rule, touch screens only, inside the phone shell: text fields are at least 16 px. Deliberately large fields such as the rider's code boxes keep their size. Desktop consoles are untouched.
4Hindi added to:
  • Home: Categories, Popular right now, Restaurants near you.
  • Search: Try searching, Popular dishes.
  • The basket bar.
  • Profile: every menu row and Sign out.
  • The vendor Menu (title, Add item, In stock / Sold out), Earnings, and the Open/Closed pill.
  • The manager home: phone order, cash, and all three counters.
5Tap targets: a new .tap-target gives an invisible 44×44 touch area to dish "Add", the +/− steppers, search suggestion chips, "Clear search" and "Clear basket". These are drawn at the same size, so a bounding-box count still lists them as small. Vendor filter chips, search tabs and promotion Pause/Delete are now 44 px tall, as are the manager's "Board" and "Sign out". Tab-bar labels went from 11 to 12 px.
6Contrast:
  • Basket bar text uses the palette's --on-accent ink, not white on orange.
  • Storefront chips sit on 60% black, and the Open pill on the photo gets a dark backing with a green dot.
  • The header Open pill, the Bestseller badge and the search counts were raised above 4.5:1.
7Item sheet is a real dialog (role="dialog", aria-modal, labelled with the dish name). Focus moves into it and Escape closes it. The address sheet also closes on Escape.
8Chart axis labels and badges went from 9–10 px to 11 px (vendor Overview and Earnings, promotions, address picker). The vendor Menu's floating "+" and its CSS were removed. The customer, vendor, rider and manager phone shells now have a <main> landmark. Category and order names wrap to two lines instead of "…".

Verified: tsc clean. ESLint shows 0 errors. QA suites unchanged: platform 35/37 (the same 2 admin-nav failures), and driver-app, sw, vendor-readiness, layering, switch and theme-color all pass.
